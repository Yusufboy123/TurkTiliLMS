# Art Direction

**Status:** Review candidate

## Identity idea

Turk Tili LMS should feel like a clear path through language: precise,
welcoming, contemporary, and culturally literate. The identity is original and
must not imitate another learning platform or default to generic SaaS gradients,
floating dashboard cards, and anonymous office stock photography.

Turkish red is a restrained recognition accent for the mark, primary action,
key path moments, and small compositional anchors. It does not flood entire
screens.

## Logo assumptions

- The primary mark must work in horizontal, compact, one-color, and favicon
  forms.
- Clear space is at least the cap height of the wordmark around the mark.
- Use approved light/dark assets; do not recolor with arbitrary admin values.
- Do not stretch, rotate, shadow, outline, or place on low-contrast imagery.
- Until a final logo is approved, use a text wordmark rather than an invented
  symbol.
- Owner upload accepts only validated logo/favicon assets and preserves an
  accessible product name.

## Visual motif

A subtle modular geometry may draw from Turkic weaving logic—interlocking paths,
repetition, and directional rhythm—without copying a protected artifact or
claiming one regional motif represents all Turkic cultures.

The same geometry can join:

- a learning path;
- paired speech forms;
- diacritic dots and language marks;
- section separators and image crops.

Motifs occupy secondary space, use low contrast, and never sit behind body text
or controls. One motif family per composition is the limit.

## Photography

- Prefer real learning moments, language exchange, teachers, books, and
  culturally varied everyday contexts.
- Use natural light, eye-level framing, and enough negative space for copy.
- Avoid staged call-center/handshake imagery, tourist clichés, and national
  symbols as decoration.
- Obtain documented consent and usage rights.
- Do not imply nationality from appearance.
- Color grading is neutral-warm; restrained red may appear as a small physical
  accent, never forced into every photograph.

## Illustration

- Use clean geometric shapes, human warmth, and simple perspective.
- Characters are diverse in age, skin tone, clothing, ability, and learning
  context without tokenism.
- Language marks, dialogue paths, cards, and modular weave geometry provide the
  shared grammar.
- Avoid mascot dependency, childish reward tropes, glossy 3D SaaS objects, and
  flags/crescents as dominant devices.
- Functional diagrams prioritize meaning over decoration.

## Course thumbnails

- Aspect ratio is 16:9 with intrinsic dimensions.
- One clear subject, one restrained geometric/path layer, and strong crop.
- Do not bake course title, level, status, or progress into the image.
- A managed set of contrast-tested tint presets may differentiate catalogs.
- Thumbnail series remain recognizable at 160 px wide and in grayscale.
- Generated images require rights/provenance review and cannot reproduce
  identifiable artists’ styles.

## Learning-path visual language

The path is directional but not gamified:

- nodes represent sections/lessons;
- line weight and shape show relationship;
- labels and icons carry state, not color alone;
- completed nodes are calm and stable;
- current node has focus-like emphasis;
- locked nodes explain the reason.

Do not use fake streak flames, trophy showers, or invented progress.

## Icons and empty states

Lucide outline icons are the functional family. Illustration does not replace
functional icons.

Empty-state illustrations use one subject, one path/motif fragment, two or three
neutral tones, and at most one restrained red accent. They remain subordinate
to the heading, explanation, and next action. Permission and error states avoid
humor.

## Marketing composition

- Lead with an outcome, credible learning context, and one primary action.
- Use asymmetric but balanced grids, generous white space, and focused imagery.
- Alternate text-led and visual-led regions; avoid endless equal-card rows.
- Evidence sections use real product content or clearly labeled prototypes.
- Decorative motif density decreases near interactive and reading regions.
- Inter is the only v1 typeface; hierarchy comes from scale, weight, and space.

## Motion personality

Motion is measured and directional: short fades, small path reveals, and spatial
drawer transitions. It communicates entry, relationship, and confirmed state.
It never bounces for routine tasks, delays content, or celebrates unverified
progress. Reduced motion removes all nonessential movement.

## Recognition rules

A composition should be recognizable through at least three of these, without
requiring a flag:

1. restrained Turkish red;
2. Inter typography with clear language-first hierarchy;
3. modular path/weave geometry;
4. authentic learning photography or consistent geometric illustration;
5. generous neutral space;
6. direct Uzbek Latin product voice.

National flags may appear only when the content itself is explicitly about a
country and the context is accurate. They are not navigation, locale, course,
or decorative devices.

## Art review gate

Before publication, every visual passes rights/provenance, cultural
appropriateness, contrast, alt-text purpose, responsive crop, dark-mode
surround, file-size, and reduced-motion review. Decorative overload, generic
stock SaaS treatment, or dominant national-symbol use blocks approval.
