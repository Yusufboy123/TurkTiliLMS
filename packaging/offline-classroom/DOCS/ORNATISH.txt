TURKTILILMS — BIR MARTALIK O‘RNATISH

1. Butun DTurkTiliLMS-TRANSFER papkasini kompyuterning C: diskiga ko‘chiring.
2. PostgreSQL 17 o‘rnatilmagan bo‘lsa, RUNTIME\installers ichidagi PostgreSQL installerni oching.
3. PostgreSQL o‘rnatishda kiritgan administrator parolini eslab qoling.
4. SETUP\FIRST-TIME-SETUP.cmd ni oching.
5. PostgreSQL administrator nomi va parolini kiriting.
6. So‘ralgan 2 yoki 3 ta o‘qituvchi hisobini kiriting. Parollar ekranda ko‘rinmaydi va matn faylga yozilmaydi.
7. Sozlash tugagach, SETUP\ALLOW-PRIVATE-FIREWALL.ps1 faylini Administrator sifatida oching.
8. SETUP\INSTALL-AUTOSTART.ps1 ni bir marta oching.
9. SETUP\START-TURKTILILMS.cmd ni oching.
10. SETUP\STATUS-TURKTILILMS.cmd orqali talabalar manzilini ko‘ring.

Muhim: APP\.env fayli maxfiy. Uni boshqa odamlarga yubormang.
Mavjud baza topilsa, setup xavfsizlik uchun to‘xtaydi va hech narsani o‘chirmaydi.
